# Design System: Droolbites

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Bakery Counter Paper"**

Droolbites is a small bakery printed on warm paper: cream background, ink-green text, one deep moss that does all the structural work, and a handful of confectionery accents (mustard, lavender, salmon, mint) that show up only as product tints, badge fills and sticker-like moments. The storefront is generous and photographic. The admin is the same material, calmer and denser, with the moss moved from accent to a full-height sidebar.

The system is a pixel-exact port of a pen.dev design at 1440px. Below that width it collapses to one column with 44px touch targets and no horizontal scroll. Where the build and the pen disagree, the pen is the spec and the code mirrors it (strokes drawn inside the box, line-heights rounded to whole pixels, Bricolage rendered at its 96 optical size at every size). Exception (owner decision 15 Nov 2026): the menu, checkout, account and admin were recomposed in code because they had too many cards and unbalanced columns; for those screens this document and the code are the spec, not the pen.

Surfaces: landing (Persuade: sticky compact header, rounded hero photo panel with the moss photo frame, USP marquee, product grids, a lavender brownies panel), menu, product page, cart drawer, 2-section checkout (details, then payment), pay page that varies by payment-method kind (QRIS, bank virtual account, e-wallet), account, and admin (Operate: moss sidebar, white cards, tables).

**Key Characteristics:**
- Moss is the single structural color: primary button, sidebar, strokes, selected states.
- Strokes are inset rings, never layout-affecting borders.
- Three radii only: 12, 20, 999.
- Flat at rest; depth comes from tonal layers (paper, oat, white, mint, tints) and hairlines.
- Display type is tight and heavy; labels are mono.

## Colors

A warm-paper neutral base with one deep green and a rotating confectionery accent set. Every saturated color comes from the logo badge: moss is the badge background (`#4e6424`, exact), and each accent is one of the paper-cut letters (D magenta, R crimson, O teal, O salmon, L lavender, B mustard, I cobalt, E lime, S orange), sampled from `public/images/site/logo-badge.png` on 1 Nov 2026. New colors must come from the logo too.

### Primary
- **Moss** (`colors.moss`): primary buttons, admin sidebar, all full strokes, active nav text, switch-on state.
- **Moss Deep** (`colors.moss-deep`): primary hover, and text on the white inverse hero button.

### Secondary
- **Mustard** (`colors.mustard`, the logo's B): the highlight. The `--highlight` token is the same mustard 2% lighter (`#dda81e`) so mustard icons on moss keep 3:1. Hero chip fill, cart count badge, cart item-count pill. Tint-mustard is the awaiting-payment badge fill.
- **Mint** (`colors.mint`): selected option tile fill, success/ready/delivery badge fill, secondary text on moss.

### Tertiary
- **Lavender, Salmon, Lime tints** (`colors.tint-lavender`, `colors.tint-salmon`, `colors.tint-lime`): large panel fills (the brownies panel is lavender) and badge fills (baking, failed/expired).
- **Accent set** (`colors.accent-*`): small decorative dots and product-category swatches only (the four hero dots, category dots). Never status dots.
- **Crimson** (`colors.crimson`): errors and invalid input stroke. **Success Green** (`colors.success-green`): success icons.

### Neutral
- **Paper** (`colors.paper`): page background and theme-color.
- **Oat** (`colors.oat`): cards, chip default fill, USP marquee band, hover fills.
- **White**: inputs, admin cards, unselected tiles, text on moss.
- **Ink** (`colors.ink`): body text and the default focus ring. **Muted Ink** (`colors.muted-ink`): helper text, table headers.
- **Strokes**: `stroke-subtle` (cards, tiles, hairlines), `stroke-divider` (summary and timeline dividers), `stroke-input` (form fields). Utilities: `border-subtle`, `border-divider`, `border-input`.

### Named Rules
**The One Structure Color Rule.** Moss carries structure; accents carry only small marks and panel tints. A screen never has two competing saturated fills.
**The Token-First Rule.** Never hard-code a hex that has a token. The one pen-stroke exception pair (`border-divider`, `border-input`) exists precisely because the pen drew them without a variable.
**The Inverse Focus Rule.** Focus ring is ink on light surfaces; any moss or photo container sets `--focus-ring: white`.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Bricolage Grotesque (variable, `opsz` axis, rendered at opsz 96 everywhere via `html`)
**Body Font:** Manrope (variable)
**Label/Mono Font:** IBM Plex Mono, 600 only, normal and italic

**Character:** A chunky, slightly quirky grotesque for voice, a clean humanist sans for reading, and a mono for prices-adjacent labels, chips and table headers.

**Full ramp:** The six named roles below are the core. The pen is laid out per frame, so it also uses intermediate display steps (24–88px, plus the 160/96px 404 numeral), a 17–20px lede/body-large, and 9–12px badge and micro-label sizes. Every one of those steps is in the frontmatter `typography` map and was copied from the pen at 1440. Before adding a new size, pick the nearest existing step.

### Hierarchy
- **Display** (700, `type-hero`: 44 / 64 / 80px, line-height 44 / 64 / 78px, tracking -1 to -2.5px): landing hero h1 only. Scaled down on 1 Nov 2026 after measuring levainbakery.com, crumblcookies.com and milkbarstore.com (their largest headings are 53–60px); the pen's 112px is retired.
- **Headline** (700): page h1s use `type-page` (40 / 56px), section h2s `type-section` (32 / 44px), checkout, pay and success h1s `type-checkout` (32 / 40px). The utilities live in `src/app/globals.css`; never set a heading size per file.
- **Title** (600-700, 20-30px, line-height 1.1-1.2): cart title, empty states, order-line names, brand name.
- **Body** (Manrope 400, 16-17px, line-height normal to 26px; 15px for secondary and admin text, 13px for helper/error): paragraphs; keep measure near 560-600px (`max-w-[560px]`/`[600px]`).
- **Label** (Plex Mono 600, 13px chips and badges, 11-12px table headers and chart axes, tracking 1px where uppercase-free eyebrow-style text exists): chips, stickers, table column headers, cart counts.
- **Button** (Bricolage 600, 15px, tracking 0.5px): all buttons and arrow links.

### Named Rules
**The Whole-Pixel Leading Rule.** Line-height is written in px, the pen's multiplier rounded half-up (14 x 1.3 gives 18px, 15 x 1.5 gives 23px) so box heights add up.
**The Display-Is-Bricolage Rule.** Headings, buttons and brand use `font-display`; never a system display face.

### Pen-mandated exception: eyebrows
The mono uppercase eyebrow above section and page titles ("SOFT COOKIES", "VISIT US", the admin date line, "MY ACCOUNT"; `src/components/content/eyebrow.tsx`, `PageHead eyebrow` in `src/components/admin/ui.tsx`) is drawn in every pen frame. The impeccable craft floor discourages kicker labels, but the owner made the pen binding at 1440 ("UI digenerate dari file pen.dev … harus sama persis"), so they stay exactly where the pen puts them: never add new ones outside the pen, and drop them only on the owner's word.

## Layout

Content sits in a `max-w-[1440px]` centered column, set once on the storefront `main`, the footer and the checkout `main` (no per-page caps); past 1440px the header's padding grows so its icons line up with that column while its hairline runs full width. Landing sections run full width inside 12px (mobile) and 24px (sm+) outer gutters; inner section padding is `px-5`/`px-10`/`px-16` stepping up by breakpoint. Vertical rhythm comes from two utilities: `section-y` (48px mobile, 80px lg) for page sections and `panel-y` (40 / 64px) inside tinted or moss panels. Product grids are 2 columns on mobile, 4 at lg (`gap-x-4 gap-y-8`, `lg:gap-6`). Admin uses a fixed 272px sticky sidebar from lg and a moss top bar with a 300px slide-in drawer below it; the main column pads 16/24/48px and stacks cards with 24px gaps. Header: always compact, 56px tall on mobile and 64px from md, with a hairline; it never changes size on scroll. On a screen under 480px tall (a phone on its side) it scrolls away with the page instead of sticking, so the header and a pinned bottom bar never take 40% of the height between them. The storefront `main` starts with a 12/24px top gutter (the landing side gutters) so the first section is not jammed under it, and sticky asides, TOCs and anchor targets sit at `top-[88px]`/`scroll-mt-[88px]`, 24px below the header.

Touch targets are 44px minimum. Visible controls smaller than that (28px stepper buttons, text links) get an invisible `after:` hit-slop pseudo-element rather than being enlarged: breadcrumbs, footer links and contact lines, accordion questions, product-card names, the FAQ contact card, contact links inside a sentence (location, policies), the menu search's clear button, the invoice's Back to order and the admin's text links all reach 40px or more. Where stacked links sit too close for that, the gap opens below lg (footer links: 20px, the pen's 14px from lg; the footer's email and phone: 20px, the pen's 4px from lg; the FAQ contact card's three links: 18px) so the areas never overlap. Placeholders are short enough to show whole in a 320px field ("Enter your email", "Street or place", "Search the menu"). The Leaflet zoom buttons are 40px. Drawers (cart, admin product form) scroll as a whole on screens under 500px tall instead of squeezing their list or form between a fixed header and footer; the product form's Save bar then sticks to the bottom. The `lg` breakpoint is the sidebar/column switch; `sm` (640px) and `md` (768px) govern header height and gutters.

### Named Rules
**The Hit-Slop Rule.** Keep the pen's visual size and extend the hit area with `after:absolute after:-inset-*`.

## Elevation & Depth

Flat by default. Depth is tonal: paper under oat under white, mint and tint panels, plus 1px hairlines drawn as inset box-shadows (`inset 0 -1px 0 stroke-subtle`) for row and header separators. The only drop shadow is a soft ink-tinted lift for floating layers (admin dropdowns, add-to-cart popover): `0 12px 32px` at 15% ink, with a tighter `0 4px 12px` variant. Overlays (cart drawer, mobile menu) use a 50% ink scrim and no shadow.

### Named Rules
**The Hairline-Not-Border Rule.** Separators are inset shadows or inset rings, so they never change a box's size.
**The Flat-Until-Floating Rule.** Only layers that float above content get a drop shadow.
**The Cutout Exception.** Photographic cookie cutouts (`Cutout` in `src/components/ui/snapshot.tsx`) carry their own `drop-shadow` (`0 10px 12px` at 22% ink) because they sit on top of a panel as objects. They are decorative only (`alt=""`, `aria-hidden`), shown from `lg` up, placed on a panel edge or corner and never over an action. Use at most two per screen.

## Shapes

Three radii: 12px for buttons, inputs, tiles and small panels (`rounded-sm`, the pen's `--radius-s`; do not use Tailwind's logical `rounded-s`), 20px for cards and large panels (`rounded-m`), and full pill (999px) for chips, badges, steppers, icon buttons and the logo badge. Full strokes are 1.25-1.5px inset rings (`inset-ring-[1.5px] inset-ring-border`; subtle 1.25px for unselected tiles; 1px for admin cards). Stickers are pills rotated -8deg (the small card/gallery variant, `Sticker size="sm"`, rotates -10deg); they are the only resting tilt on the site, and the rating stickers (Google Maps rating, product rating) stand straight so the numbers read at a glance. Full-bleed panels with content over the photo (landing hero, auth photo panel) keep only their 20px radius, no ring. Every other photo sits flat in the one shared frame, `photoFrame` in `src/components/ui/snapshot.tsx` (also used by `Snapshot`): 20px radius and no outline, since the photo's oat ground already separates it from the paper (owner decision 2026-11-01; rings stay on interactive things: buttons, inputs, chips, stickers, choice tiles). Thumbnails under about 80px use `rounded-sm` and round avatars `rounded-pill`. The landing brownies image sits flat on the oat plate in the same frame. Decorative `Cutout` cookies, the logo badge and icons get no frame and no tilt. Functional rotation stays (chevrons and accordion icons turning on open). Icons are lucide line icons at 18-24px.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Shape:** 12px radius, 16px / 28px padding, Bricolage 600 15px, tracking 0.5px.
- **Primary:** moss fill, white text; hover moss-deep.
- **Secondary:** transparent, ink text, 1.5px moss inset ring; hover oat fill.
- **Inverse:** white fill on moss or photo surfaces; hover oat. On the hero, secondary is recolored white with a white ring and a white 10% hover.
- **Focus:** the 2px ring shows on `:focus-visible` only. A drawer or dialog opened from the keyboard puts focus on its first control (with the ring); opened with a pointer or by the URL (`?cart=open`) it focuses the dialog itself, so no ring sits on the cart's close button for a mouse user, and Tab starts at the first control.
- **Arrow link:** Bricolage 600 15px with a 1.25px moss underline drawn as an inset shadow and a lucide arrow that nudges 2px on hover.
- **Casing:** button labels are uppercase, written so in the strings ("ADD PRODUCT", "EXPORT CSV", "PREVIOUS"), in the storefront and the admin, including the small in-row buttons and pagination; arrow links, text links and chips stay sentence case. An icon carries a "+", not the label. Admin page-head actions are one size (`min-h-11 px-5 py-3`, secondary for the second action); `smallButtonClasses` is only for in-row actions (Edit, Rename).
- Disabled: 50% opacity, not-allowed cursor.

### Chips, Stickers, Status Badges
- **Chip:** pill, oat fill, Plex Mono 600 13px, 8/14px padding. Hero chip overrides to mustard.
- **Sticker:** pill, paper fill, 1.25px moss ring, rotated -8deg; rating stickers sit at 0deg.
- **Status badge:** pill, tinted fill, 13px 600 label, no dot (owner rule, 28 Oct 2026: the site shows no status dots anywhere, including open/closed indicators and admin status filters; the pen still draws them). Tone map: tint-mustard awaiting payment, new order and awaiting confirmation, mint paid/completed/ready/out for delivery, lavender baking, salmon failed/expired/cancelled, oat new/hidden.
- **Stock** (owner decision 16 Nov 2026: a number per size, empty = not tracked; it drops when an order is paid and comes back when the store declines or cancels it). The storefront only speaks up at 5 or fewer: `StockBadge` (`src/components/shop/stock.tsx`), a small Plex Mono 11px pill, mustard tint "Only 3 left" / "Sisa 3", oat "Sold out" / "Habis". It sits under the description on a one-size card, after the name on the banner size rows, on the size tile's second line (size modal, product page, home brownies) and beside the price on a one-size product page. A sold-out size tile is disabled and the picker starts on the first size in stock; the stepper stops at what's left. A product with every size sold out swaps its photo sticker for an oat "Sold out", gets an oat disabled "+" and a disabled "SOLD OUT" button. Untracked or more than 5 left: nothing shown. Cart drawer lines that ran short say so in crimson 13px under the meta ("Only 2 left. Lower the quantity…"), the last units get a muted "That's all 4 we have left.", and the footer adds one crimson line; checkout refuses with the short items listed and a "Fix my cart" link to `/menu?cart=open`, which opens the drawer. Admin: products list Stock column (total left, or a mustard "Low stock" / salmon "Sold out" status badge with what's low on a muted line under it; "Sold out" only when every size is tracked and at 0, since an untracked size can still be ordered; "Not tracked" muted), a Stock input (88px) after each size's price in the product form with one hint line, a "Running low" list in the overview's Your menu pane (mono label, rows linking to the product with the same badges) and, for paid orders that came up short, a salmon banner under the orders banner plus a salmon alert and a crimson "only 0 of 1 were in stock" line on the order page.

### Cards / Containers
- Storefront cards: oat fill, 20px radius, no border. Admin `Card`: white, 20px radius, 1px subtle inset ring.
- **The One Surface Rule** (owner decision 15 Nov 2026: "kebanyakan card dan ga seimbang"). A container must earn its place: no card inside a card, no card that wraps a single number or label, and related facts share one surface split by hairlines (`shadow-[inset_0_-1px_0_var(--border-subtle)]`) instead of sitting in separate boxes. Columns on the same row end at similar heights; a short side column is sticky rather than leaving a hole. Say each fact once per screen (an order number, method or total appears in one place, not in the heading, a status card and a details list).
- **No wedged cards.** A grid of real items holds only real items: no promo, CTA, filler or decorative card to plug a hole or pad a column. The menu has one group per filter category, in filter order, all on the same column tracks (2 columns below lg, 4 from lg) so every card is the same size; from lg small groups share a row (packed in filter order, the last group in a row stretching to the edge), and a single-product group is a landscape `banner` card two columns wide (photo left; from sm a multi-size product lists its sizes and prices on hairline rows, bottom-aligned with the price row so spare height sits under the description; from lg to xl the rows drop the serving note to stay on one line). Search and category results start at the left edge, under the search field. The menu toolbar is one row from lg: the search field fills the left and the categories sit beside it as a segmented control in one white 48px capsule (logo-colour dot, live match count); below lg the chips scroll sideways under the field (owner decision 2026-11-02). Menu group headings carry no count or unit line. Say each store fact once, in its home (hours in the announcement bar and /location, freshness in the hero, rating in the review band and product pages, ordering steps in the FAQ); checkout carries no security reassurance copy.
- Cart drawer: paper throughout; slides in, fades under reduced motion. Each line is a hairline-separated row: a 72px `rounded-sm` thumbnail, the name (Bricolage 17px) with the line total on its right, one muted line for the size and, from two, the unit price ("Rp5.000 each"), then the 28px stepper with a trash icon button on the far right (labelled "Remove <name>"). The footer is flat on the paper under a hairline (no oat slab): Subtotal with a Bricolage 22px amount, one delivery line ("Plus delivery from Rp10.000, or pick up for free."), the full-width primary Checkout button and a Continue shopping text button. The empty cart is one centred message (96px cookie, title, one line, See the menu) and nothing else.

### Payment service fee
The gateway may add a service fee the customer pays (who bears it is a gateway account setting; the amount depends on the channel: flat for a bank VA, a percentage for QRIS) and names it only in its answer to a charge. So the Payment step never promises a total that can change: while a fee may apply (`serviceFeeMayApply`: a provider that can charge one, unless its latest charge came back with the store bearing the fee) the button says PLACE ORDER instead of PAY Rp…, with one muted line under it: "A payment service fee may be added, depending on the method. You'll see the exact amount to pay on the next step, before you pay." The change-method form carries the same kind of line. From the charge on, every place the customer sees money shows the amount charged (`payable()`: the total plus the fee): the pay page's Amount to pay with "Includes a payment service fee of …", the Service fee line in the order summary (pay, order and success pages), the account history row, and the emails. The admin orders list and the overview's Recent orders show that same amount, with a muted "incl. Rp4.000 fee" line on the list; the order page's Items keep the order total (the store's money) and Payment lists Amount and Service fee. The CSV has Order total, Payment service fee and Amount charged columns. Sales report revenue counts order totals only (the fee goes to the gateway). The mock provider charges no fee unless `MOCK_SERVICE_FEE` (rupiah, flat) is set, for trying these lines locally.

### Payment minimums
The gateway's channel list carries no amounts, so minimums are learned: a charge refused with AMOUNT_BELOW_MINIMUM records the method's floor in memory for 6 hours (its minimum if the answer names one, else "more than this amount"; a refusal for one virtual-account bank marks every bank, since a customer refused by one would hit the same error at the next), and a later accepted charge at or below it clears that method. The floors live in process memory, so a restart forgets them until the next refusal (persisting them needs a table). The Payment step and the change-method form then show that method's tile disabled with "Min. Rp10.000" (or "Not for an order this small") in place of its hint, the default method skips it, and a refused charge says the total is below the method's minimum instead of "not available right now". The mock provider has no minimums.

### Motion
Durations 150-300ms, `ease-out`, on color, transform and opacity only. Photo hover zoom is one recipe everywhere (menu cards, FAQ and 404 picks): `scale-[1.05]` over 300ms. ## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** reach for tokens: `bg-primary`, `bg-card`, `text-muted-foreground`, `inset-ring-border`, `rounded-sm|m|pill`.
- **Do** draw strokes as inset rings or inset shadows so sizes match the design.
- **Do** write line-height in whole px and letter-spacing in px.
- **Do** set `[--focus-ring:var(--white)]` on moss and photo containers; use `outline-hidden`, never `outline-none`.
- **Do** use `cn()` (tailwind-merge) so a later class wins; use `Button`/`ButtonLink`/`Chip`/`Field`/`StatusBadge` rather than re-styling.
- **Do** give every new animation a `motion-reduce:` variant.
- **Do** keep prices as `Rp` plus dot thousands and product names in English.

### Don't:
- **Don't** use `border-*` for pen strokes; it resizes the box.
- **Don't** use `rounded-s` (logical corner); the 12px radius is `rounded-sm`.
- **Don't** add a fourth radius or a second saturated structural color.
- **Don't** put drop shadows on resting cards.
- **Don't** hard-code a hex that has a token, or edit `globals.css` casually (Turbopack does not hot-reload it reliably).
- **Don't** shrink a control below 44px of hit area on mobile; add hit-slop instead.
